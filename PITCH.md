# Peerly: pitch cheat-sheet

**One-liner:** Verified-student network: find friends and teammates, trade items, sell skills, all with open-source AI and realtime chat.

## Criteria map
- **Open-source AI:** Qwen3 (Ollama) for assistant, listing/skill-card generation, icebreakers; MiniLM embeddings (in-browser) for semantic search and people matching.
- **Realtime database:** Supabase Postgres Realtime (messages, listings) + Presence (live students).
- **Social good:** campus loneliness + student side-income.

## 2-minute demo
1. Open two devices/tabs. Onboard both: each sees the other as a LIVE student.
2. Discover: swipe, match, confetti, DM in realtime.
3. Campus Lobby: group chat across both screens.
4. Market -> Skills -> type "someone to edit a reel" -> tap ✦ (semantic ranking).
5. List an item: describe it in one sentence, AI writes the card, Publish; it appears on the other device instantly.
6. AI tab: "laptop under 60k for coding".
7. Close with the roadmap: campus SSO auth, moderation, payments/escrow, vector DB.
